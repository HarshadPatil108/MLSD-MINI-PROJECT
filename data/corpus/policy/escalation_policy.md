# Incident Severity and Escalation Routing Policy

## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines unambiguous criteria for incident severity classification (`SEV1` through `SEV4`) and operational routing targets for advisory recommendations generated by the IncidentOps assistant.

## Severity Definitions

### SEV1 — Critical Production Outage
- **Definition:** Total loss of core database functionality, data corruption risk, or catastrophic disk space exhaustion (< 5% free space remaining, imminent read-only filesystem switch).
- **Impact:** Customer-facing services completely unavailable; core revenue streams halted.
- **Initial Target:** `incident-commander`
- **Response Expectation:** Immediate war room assembly; paging secondary DBAs and engineering leads.

### SEV2 — Major Production Degradation
- **Definition:** Severe performance impairment, connection pool exhaustion (> 85%), high deadlock frequency (> 5/sec) causing user transactions to fail, or replication lag threatening RPO targets.
- **Impact:** Significant customer disruption or partial feature unavailability.
- **Initial Target:** `page-secondary` or `self-serve runbook` (if high-confidence remediation available).
- **Response Expectation:** On-call response within 15 minutes.

### SEV3 — Moderate Degradation / Warning
- **Definition:** Elevated query latency, abnormal resource utilization (CPU or memory > 80%), or single replica lag without immediate data loss risk.
- **Impact:** Degraded system performance without direct user-facing error spike.
- **Initial Target:** `self-serve runbook`
- **Response Expectation:** Standard on-call triage within 1 hour.

### SEV4 — Informational Anomaly
- **Definition:** Minor metric deviation, background maintenance alert, or non-urgent capacity planning notification.
- **Impact:** Negligible operational impact.
- **Initial Target:** `self-serve runbook`
- **Response Expectation:** Next business day or async ticket review.

## Routing Targets and Decision Criteria

### Target 1: `self-serve runbook`
- **Criteria:**
  - High confidence root cause identification matching an approved operational runbook.
  - All proposed remediation steps are strictly non-destructive diagnostic or safe recovery procedures with verified citations.
  - No conflicting symptoms or ambiguous diagnostic indicators.
- **Action:** Deliver structured runbook diagnosis, diagnostic queries, and advisory steps directly to the on-call engineer.

### Target 2: `page-secondary`
- **Criteria:**
  - Ambiguous diagnostic evidence: symptoms match multiple candidate cause classes with similar likelihood.
  - Complex cascading failure: connection saturation coupled with lock contention or replica disconnection.
  - Proposed remediation steps require secondary peer review or failover decision.
- **Action:** Page secondary database on-call engineer for collaborative investigation.

### Target 3: `incident-commander`
- **Criteria:**
  - Any SEV1 incident.
  - Imminent or ongoing data loss or unrecoverable disk saturation.
  - Inter-team communication required across infrastructure, security, or product teams.
- **Action:** Page Incident Commander and declare production incident.
